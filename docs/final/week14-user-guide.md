# User Guide: FitTrack Activity Logger

## Who Is This For?

This guide is for anyone who wants to use the FitTrack app to log their workouts. It is made for regular users. No specialized technical skills are needed.

## What Can You Do?

With FitTrack, you can:

- Log new workouts (like Running, Weightlifting, or Cycling)
- See your past workout history for the applicable week
- Edit or delete any workout if you made a mistake

The app runs locally in your web browser and works without an internet connection once it is loaded.

## How to Use FitTrack

### Step 1: Open the App

Go to the main page of the app in your browser. No login is needed.

### Step 2: Add a Workout

1. Click the "Log Workout" button.
2. Choose the type of workout (e.g., Yoga, Swimming, Cycling).
3. Enter the date, how long it lasted, and any notes (like distance or equipment used).
4. Click “Save.” The workout will appear in your list.

### Step 3: View Your Workouts

All saved workouts for the applicable week will show up on the main screen in order from newest to oldest. You can scroll through them anytime.

### Step 4: Edit or Delete a Workout

To make changes:

- Find the workout in your list.
- Click the "Edit" button.
- Update the info and click “Save” again.
- To remove it, click “Delete” and confirm.

## Known Limitations

Right now, the app has some limits because we focused on core features:

- Your data is saved only in your current browser. If you switch devices or clear your browser data, your workouts will be gone.
- There is no account system. The existing version does not support logging in or syncing across phones or computers.
- You cannot export your data to a file (like CSV or PDF) as currently implemented.
- The design is simple and works well as intended. Although it still must be thoroughly tested on very small phone screens to ensure functionality.
